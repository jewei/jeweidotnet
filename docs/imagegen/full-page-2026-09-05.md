## Final cover prompt

```text
Use case: compositing.
Asset type: editorial blog cover for jewei.net, matching the existing project covers.
Primary request: Make a finished Full Page cover. Full Page is the user's Brave extension that captures a complete page, shows a local preview, and saves a PNG.
Input images: image 1 is the real Full Page screenshot preview and defines the product interface. Image 2 is the TypeID cover and defines the site's paper color, typography, spacing, and restrained green accents.
Output: one 1600 x 1000 landscape image, 16:10 composition.
Composition: two balanced columns on warm off-white paper. Keep all meaningful content inside the central 72% of the width with at least 14% blank space on EACH side and at least 14% above and below. On the left, a large black serif title "Full Page", a smaller sage-green subtitle "The whole page. One PNG.", a fine short green rule, and the small label "A screenshot extension for Brave". Put a small "jewei.net" signature beneath. On the right, a complete compact preview window based on image 1, including the real blue Full Page icon, the tall captured "A day by the sea" sample page, and the blue "Download PNG" button. Preserve the actual interface character and its blue controls. Keep every window edge and title letter well inside the frame. Do not stretch or cut the sample page.
Style: quiet technical editorial design consistent with image 2. Warm cream paper, black serif title, sage accents outside the screenshot, soft restrained window shadow. The Full Page interface remains a light neutral preview with its real blue controls.
Text verbatim outside the product preview: "Full Page"; "The whole page. One PNG."; "A screenshot extension for Brave"; "jewei.net".
Constraints: one finished cover, not a screenshot of the blog. No invented extension features, no store badge, no claims of store availability, no Chrome logo, no extra product names. No large decorative shapes, neon glow, saturated gradients, outer frame, crop marks, or watermark. Give both columns enough spare space that 16:10 cards and a centered social-image crop keep all content visible.
```
