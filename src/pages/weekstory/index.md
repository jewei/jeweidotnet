**Weekstory** shows your life as a calm grid of weeks, one small dot for each. Moments from world history sit on the weeks they happened, so you can read your own story beside the world's. You can write one short line for any week, or keep a moment from that week as its line, and colour the chapters of your life.

Weekstory has no account and no server. Your story stays on your phone. Read the **[Weekstory privacy policy](/weekstory/privacy/)** for the details.

## Your story and your data

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone only: your birthday, the horizon you choose, your lines, and your chapters. To keep a copy, open **Settings → Export a copy**. It writes one file you can save or send wherever you like. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

## Moments from history

The moments are written into the app and do not need a network. They count once they fall on or after your birthday. To hide a kind of moment, such as Sport or Technology, use the switches under **Settings → Moments from the world**.

## The one-time unlock

The free story keeps up to three chapters. A one-time purchase, made through the App Store, lifts that limit on your iPhone. If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, open **Settings → Restore purchase**.
