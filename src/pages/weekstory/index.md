**Weekstory** shows your life as a calm grid of weeks, one small dot for each. Add the moments of your own story to the weeks they happened. With the one-time unlock, moments from world history appear beside yours, so you can read your story beside the world's.

Weekstory has no account and no server. Your story stays on your phone. Read the **[Weekstory privacy policy](/weekstory/privacy/)** for the details.

## Your story and your data

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone only: your birthday, the horizon you choose, and your moments. To keep a copy, open **Settings → Export a copy**. It writes one file you can save or send wherever you like. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

## Moments from history

The moments from world history are written into the app and do not need a network. They count once they fall on or after your birthday. To hide a kind of moment, such as Sport or Technology, use the switches under **Settings → Moments from the world**.

## The one-time unlock

Your own moments are free. A one-time purchase, made through the App Store, shows the moments from world history on your weeks and lets you add them to your story. If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, open **Settings → Restore purchase**.
