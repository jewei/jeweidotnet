_Last updated: November 25, 2026._

This policy covers **Weekstory**, an iPhone app made by **Jewei Mak**. Weekstory keeps your story on your phone.

## What Weekstory stores

- Your birthday and the horizon you choose.
- The lines you write for weeks, and the chapters you name.
- Your choice of light or dark, and which kinds of history moments to show.
- Whether you have bought the one-time unlock.

All of this is stored on your device only. There is no account. Weekstory has no server and does not send your story anywhere.

## What Weekstory collects

Nothing. Weekstory has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

## History moments

The moments from world history are written into the app. Weekstory does not fetch them, and it does not record which ones you view or choose.

## Sharing and export

When you share the picture of your weeks or export a copy of your story, Weekstory makes a file on your phone and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice, and the app you send it to has its own terms. Weekstory removes its temporary copy once the share sheet has it. Saving the picture to Photos asks for permission to add photos only.

## Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Weekstory receives only whether the purchase succeeded.

## Children

Weekstory collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Weekstory stored.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. For a privacy question, email **[jewei@duck.com](mailto:jewei@duck.com)**.
