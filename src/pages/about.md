## Engineer

I have spent more than 15 years working on backend systems, product architecture, APIs, payments, and infrastructure. The systems have ranged from early product experiments to services used by more than a million people.

Laravel and PHP have been steady tools through much of that work. So have Cloudflare, AWS, distributed services, and the less glamorous work of making data and money move safely between systems.

These days, I spend less time handcrafting every line and more time designing good boundaries, steering AI-assisted workflows, and reviewing what the machines produce. Speed is useful. Clean architecture, thoughtful developer experience, and systems that age well still matter more.

## Builder

I like small tools with a clear job. [Claude Meter](/projects/#claude-meter), [Bopop](/projects/#bopop), [Uppa](/projects/#uppa), and [TypeID PHP](/projects/#typeid-php) started with problems I wanted to solve or questions I wanted to test.

Some become open-source packages. Some become macOS utilities. Some remain experiments. Each one teaches me something that client work does not.

## Writer

Writing is how I slow the work down enough to understand it. I write about architecture, software scale, PHP, tools, and the changing practice of building with AI. The useful notes live in [Writing](/blog/).

I try to publish details that another engineer can use. This means commands, trade-offs, mistakes, and results instead of general advice.

## How I think

Personality tests are imperfect shorthand, but the shorthand is sometimes useful. Myers-Briggs calls me an [assertive INTJ architect](https://www.16personalities.com/articles/assertive-architect-intj-a-vs-turbulent-architect-intj-t?ref=jewei.net). The Enneagram calls me a [5w6 investigator](https://enneagramuniverse.com/enneagram/learn/enneagram-wings/enneagram_5w6/?ref=jewei.net). Both descriptions point to the same habit: I like taking tangled problems apart and putting them back together as clear systems.
